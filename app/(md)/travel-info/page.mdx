## Getting to Malaysia

### Airports

**Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL)**
- Main international airport serving Kuala Lumpur
- Located about 45km from Sunway University
- Best option for international travelers
- Direct flights from major cities worldwide

**Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah Airport (SZB) - Subang**
- Smaller airport, mainly serves domestic and regional flights
- Located about 15km from Sunway University
- Closer to the venue but limited international connections

### Visa Requirements

- Check Malaysia's visa requirements for your nationality
- Many countries have visa-free entry or visa-on-arrival options
- Contact us for invitation letters if needed for visa applications

## Getting to Sunway University

### From Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL)

**By KLIA Express + LRT (Recommended)**
1. Take KLIA Express to KL Sentral (28-35 minutes)
2. Transfer to LRT Kelana Jaya Line
3. Take LRT to USJ 7 Station (15 minutes)
4. Short taxi/Grab ride to Sunway University (5 minutes)

**By Airport Bus**
1. Take Aerobus to Sunway Pyramid (direct service)
2. Walk or take shuttle to Sunway University (5 minutes)

**By Taxi/Grab**
- Direct ride: 45-60 minutes (depending on traffic)
- Cost: RM 80-120

### From Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah Airport (SZB)

**By Taxi/Grab**
- Direct ride: 20-30 minutes
- Cost: RM 30-50

**By Public Transport**
1. Take RapidKL bus to nearby LRT station
2. Connect to LRT system to reach USJ 7 Station

### From Kuala Lumpur City Center

**By LRT (Recommended)**
1. Take LRT Kelana Jaya Line to USJ 7 Station
2. Short taxi/Grab ride to Sunway University

**By BRT Sunway Line**
1. Connect to BRT Sunway Line at various points
2. Take BRT to Sunway University Station (direct)

## Local Transportation

### Ride-Hailing Apps
- **Grab**: Most popular and reliable
- **InDrive**: Alternative option

### Public Transport
- **LRT (Light Rail Transit)**: Efficient for city travel
- **BRT Sunway Line**: Direct connection to Sunway area
- **RapidKL buses**: Extensive bus network

### Taxis
- Available but less convenient than ride-hailing apps
- Negotiate fare beforehand or insist on meter

## Accommodation

### Near Sunway University
- **Sunway Resort Hotel & Spa**: Premium option within Sunway City
- **Sunway Pyramid Hotel**: Mid-range, connected to shopping mall
- **Budget hotels**: Various options in nearby Subang Jaya and USJ areas

### Kuala Lumpur City Center
- Wider selection of hotels
- About 30-45 minutes travel time to venue
- Good option if you plan to explore KL

## Currency & Payments

- **Currency**: Malaysian Ringgit (MYR/RM)
- **Cards**: Widely accepted at hotels, restaurants, and shopping centers
- **Cash**: Recommended for local transport and street food
- **ATMs**: Readily available throughout Malaysia

## Useful Apps

- **Grab**: For transportation and food delivery
- **Touch 'n Go eWallet**: For public transport and payments
- **Google Maps**: Navigation and public transport directions
- **Google Translate**: Supports Malay language

## Language

- **Official Languages**: Bahasa Malaysia, English
- **Widely Spoken**: English is commonly used in business and tourism
- **Other Languages**: Chinese dialects, Tamil

## Weather

November in Malaysia is typically:
- **Temperature**: 24-32°C (75-90°F)
- **Humidity**: High (70-90%)
- **Rainfall**: Possible afternoon showers
- **Clothing**: Light, breathable fabrics recommended

## Cultural Tips

- Malaysia is a multicultural country with Malay, Chinese, and Indian influences
- Dress modestly when visiting religious sites
- Tipping is not mandatory but appreciated
- Food is halal-certified in most establishments
