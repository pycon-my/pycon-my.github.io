# PyCon MY 2025 is now HRDC Claimable!

Dear HR,

Greetings from Python Conference Malaysia (PyCon MY), an annual conference focused on the Python programming language by the grassroots community.

This year, PyCon MY 2025 is scheduled to take place on November 1st and 2nd (Saturday and Sunday) at Sunway University, Selangor, Malaysia.

This is the 11th year of PyCon MY, and we are striving to collaborate more with academic institutions, government-linked agencies, communities, and industries.

You may apply for the grant through the [HRDC etris website](https://etris.hrdcorp.gov.my/DigiGov/login.jsp). 

Should you need further assistance, please feel free to contact us at [board@pycon.my](mailto:board@pycon.my).

Please note that approval may take a few days, so we encourage early application.

Thank you.
