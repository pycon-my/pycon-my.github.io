# Sponsor PyCon Malaysia 2025

To build a stronger future for the Python community in Malaysia, starting in 2025, PyCon MY would like to focus on the following areas:

- **Events**: To hold more satellite events aside from the signature annual PyCon MY, preferably in major cities outside Kuala Lumpur.
- **Community**: To provide more platforms for the community to showcase their talents and get spotted. 
- **Collaboration**: To form more allies with communities within and outside of Malaysia.

Your valued support in these domains will significantly contribute to the success and impact of PyCon MY. We aim to provide our patrons and collaborators the opportunities to do the following:

- **Recruitment**: Get in touch with the local and international community and talent scout. 
- **Showcasing**: To showcase your products and services to the PyCon MY community.
- **Community Outreach**: To reach out to more communities.
- **Support Non-profit Conference**: PyCon is a not-for-profit conference run entirely by volunteers dedicated to growing the Python developer community. Sponsorship is vital in keeping the costs of attending the conference affordable for everyone.
